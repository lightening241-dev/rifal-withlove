What we'll help with

Four kinds of support, each shaped around you. Use one, or combine them to suit your plan.

Daily living

Help with everyday tasks, so you can stay independent and comfortable at home.

  • Personal care
  • Cooking and meals
  • Household jobs
  • Daily routines

Community access

Get out and do the things you enjoy, with someone by your side.

  • Outings and hobbies
  • Groups and events
  • Shopping and errands
  • Making friends

Transport

Safe, reliable rides to the places you need and want to go.

  • Appointments
  • Work and study
  • Visiting family
  • Community activities

Accommodation and respite

A safe place to live or stay, with support whenever you need it.

  • Supported Independent Living (SIL)
  • Short-term stays
  • Respite for carers
  • 24/7 support options

We treat every person we support the way we'd want our own family to be treated: with patience, respect, and genuine care.

How it works

Getting started is simple, and you're in charge the whole way.

  1. Get in touch

    Fill in the form below, call or email us. A family member, carer or support coordinator can contact us for you.

  2. Meet and talk

    We'll get to know you, your goals and what matters most to you.

  3. Plan your support

    Together we agree on what support you'll get, when, and who will provide it.

  4. Start your support

    Your support begins. We check in regularly and adjust it whenever you need.

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.

When will Rifal Withlove open?

We're getting ready now. Register your interest and we'll contact you as soon as we're able to start supporting new participants.

Can I use my NDIS funding for your services?

Our services are designed for NDIS participants. How you can use your funding depends on your plan and how it's managed. Get in touch and we'll talk it through with you.

Can a family member or support coordinator contact you for me?

Yes. Family members, carers, guardians and support coordinators are all welcome to contact us on your behalf.

Do I have to commit to anything by registering interest?

No. Registering your interest just means we'll contact you when we open. There's no cost and no commitment.

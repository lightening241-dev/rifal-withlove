Support at home and in your community, from people who care.

Rifal Withlove is a new NDIS support provider. We help people with disability live the way they choose, with support that fits their plan, their goals and their everyday life.

Get in touch See our services

What we'll help with

Four kinds of support, each shaped around you. You can use one, or combine them.

  • Daily living

    Help with everyday tasks, so you can stay independent and comfortable at home.

    • Personal care and hygiene
    • Cooking and meals
    • Cleaning and household jobs
    • Building routines that work for you

  • Community access

    Get out and do the things you enjoy, with someone by your side.

    • Social outings and hobbies
    • Groups, classes and events
    • Shopping and errands
    • Building friendships and confidence

  • Transport

    Safe, reliable rides to the places you need and want to go.

    • Medical and therapy appointments
    • Work, study and day programs
    • Visiting family and friends
    • Community activities

  • Accommodation and respite

    A safe place to live or stay, with support when you need it.

    • Supported Independent Living (SIL)
    • Short-term accommodation
    • Respite for you and your carers
    • Support available around the clock

How it will work

Getting started is simple, and you're in charge the whole way.

  1. Get in touch

    Call or email us. A family member, carer or support coordinator can contact us for you.

  2. Meet and talk

    We'll get to know you, your goals and what matters to you.

  3. Plan your support

    Together we agree on what support you'll get, when, and who will provide it.

  4. Start your support

    Your support begins. We check in regularly and adjust it whenever you need.
